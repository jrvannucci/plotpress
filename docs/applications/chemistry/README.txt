.. _app_chemistry:

Chemistry and spectroscopy
--------------------------

Separations, spectra, correlation maps and electrochemistry. Spectroscopy has
strong plotting conventions that are worth honouring even though they look
backwards -- infrared runs right to left in wavenumber, NMR runs backwards in
ppm, transmittance is read downward -- and the examples show how to get them
with axis inversion rather than by pre-flipping the data.
