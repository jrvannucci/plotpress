.. _app_earth:

Earth, ocean and atmosphere
---------------------------

Remote sensing, weather radar, climate records and subsurface geophysics. The
recurring problems here are incomplete coverage (land under an ocean sensor, a
radar's unfilled corners), quantities whose sign carries the meaning (radial
velocity toward or away, an anomaly above or below a reference), and grids that
are polar, curvilinear or unevenly spaced before they ever reach the plotting
call.
