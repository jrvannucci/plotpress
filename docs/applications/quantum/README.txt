.. _app_quantum:

Quantum devices and atomic physics
----------------------------------

Two-tone spectroscopy, charge stability diagrams, Wigner functions and the decay
curves that calibration runs produce. Most of these are two-dimensional parameter
sweeps -- drive one knob along x, another along y, record a response -- which is
the single most common shape a device-physics measurement takes, and the one that
most rewards getting the colour normalisation right.
