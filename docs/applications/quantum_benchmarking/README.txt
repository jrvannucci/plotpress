.. _app_quantum_benchmarking:

Benchmarking and multi-qubit
-------------------------------

System-level validation once individual gates are calibrated: randomized,
purity and cross-entropy benchmarking, leakage-aware variants, and the
multi-qubit signatures -- GHZ parity, a Bell/CHSH correlation map, a
process's full Pauli transfer matrix, a repetition-code syndrome -- that only
show up once several qubits act together.
