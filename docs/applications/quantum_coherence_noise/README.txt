Coherence and noise
---------------------

How a qubit loses information, and to what: T1/T2 decay, Ramsey dephasing
against a wandering environment, a two-level-system defect drifting through
resonance, and the noise-bias engineering (dynamical decoupling, a
Kerr-cat's protected subspace) built to outlast it.
