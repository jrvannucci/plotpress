Real-world use cases
--------------------

Measurements as they actually arrive from instruments and simulations, across
geophysics, climate, astronomy, medical imaging, fluids, materials,
spectroscopy and quantum devices.

Each starts from the data and lets the colour treatment follow from it: what
the sign means, how many decades it spans, which cells are missing, and whether
the sampling grid is rectilinear at all. Every figure below is also **live** --
pick a tool on the embedded copy and zoom, pan, point-pick or annotate it.
